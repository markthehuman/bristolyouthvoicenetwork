Bristol Youth Voice Network

About the Network

The Bristol Youth Voice Network is a free and informal network that brings together organisations and individuals who are committed to strengthening youth voice, participation and influence across Bristol.

Membership provides an opportunity to connect with others working across the youth, community, voluntary and public sectors, share learning and stay informed about developments relevant to children and young people's participation.
