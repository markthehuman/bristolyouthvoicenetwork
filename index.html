About the Network

The Bristol Youth Voice Network is a free and informal network that brings together organisations and individuals who are committed to strengthening youth voice, participation and influence across Bristol.

Membership provides an opportunity to connect with others working across the youth, community, voluntary and public sectors, share learning and to stay informed about developments relevant to children and young people's participation.

Who Can Join?

Membership is open to:

  • Voluntary and community organisations
  • Charities
  • Schools, colleges and education providers
  • Public sector organisations
  • Health organisations
  • Youth services and youth groups
  • Social enterprises
  • Individuals with a professional interest in youth participation and engagement

Join the Network

If you would like to become a member of the Bristol Youth Voice Network, please click on the button below. Together, we can help create more opportunities for children and young people across Bristol to have their voices heard and influence the decisions that affect their lives.

Events

The Network organises a small number of meetings, events and learning opportunities throughout the year. These may include:

  • Networking events
  • Guest speakers and presentations
  • Good practice sharing sessions
  • Workshops and training opportunities
  • Discussions on emerging issues affecting children and young people
  • Collaborative planning and partnership development

Participation at events is voluntary and members can choose which activities they would like to attend.
