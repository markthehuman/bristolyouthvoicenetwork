Why
the Network Exists

The Bristol Youth Voice Network brings together organisations and individuals who are passionate about ensuring young people's voices are heard, valued and acted upon. Through collaboration, shared learning and mutual support, we work to strengthen youth participation and influence across Bristol.

Bristol Youth Voice Network Continues to Grow

Membership provides an opportunity to connect with others working across the youth, community, voluntary and public sectors, share learning and stay informed about developments relevant to children and young people's participation.

The Bristol Youth Voice Network continues to go from strength to strength, bringing together people and organisations who are committed to amplifying young people's voices and strengthening participation across the city.

We are pleased to share that the network now has 113 registered members, representing over 50 organisations from across Bristol and the wider West of England.

The network includes colleagues from:

  • Youth and play organisations
  • Community and voluntary sector organisations
  • Arts and cultural organisations
  • Health services
  • Education and skills providers
  • Bristol City Council
  • Universities and research partners
  • Equalities, inclusion and advocacy organisations

Cross-Sector Community of Practice

The Bristol Youth Voice Network exists to connect practitioners, organisations and young people who are passionate about meaningful participation.

Through the network, members:

  • Share learning, opportunities and good practice
  • Build partnerships and collaborative projects
  • Showcase youth-led work
  • Explore innovative approaches to participation
  • Support workforce development
  • Strengthen the influence of young people's voices in decision-making

The diversity of organisations involved is one of the network's greatest strengths, creating opportunities for youth voice to influence not only youth services, but also health, education, culture, community development and wider civic life across Bristol.

Looking Ahead

As the network continues to grow, our focus remains on creating opportunities for organisations to connect, learn from one another and work together to ensure that young people's voices help shape services, opportunities and decisions that affect their lives.

Thank you to everyone who has contributed to the network's development so far. We look forward to welcoming even more organisations and practitioners over the coming year.
