Events

The Network organises a small number of meetings, events and learning opportunities throughout the year. These may include:


  • Networking events
  • Guest speakers and presentations
  • Good practice sharing sessions
  • Workshops and training opportunities
  • Discussions on emerging issues affecting children and young people
  • Collaborative planning and partnership development

If you would like to become a member of the Bristol Youth Voice Network, please click on the button below. Together, we can help create more opportunities for children and young people across Bristol to have their voices heard and influence the decisions that affect their lives.
