Why join the Network?

When children and young people are actively involved in decision-making, organisations are better able to understand their experiences, respond to their needs and design services that work for them.

The Bristol Youth Voice Network supports a collaborative approach to youth participation, helping members to:

  • Share knowledge, ideas and effective practice
  • Learn from participation and engagement activity across Bristol
  • Build connections with organisations and professionals who share a commitment to youth voice
  • Promote opportunities for children and young people to get involved
  • Increase the influence of young people in local decision-making
  • Stay informed about developments, funding and opportunities in the sector

Who can join?

Membership is open to anyone with an interest in youth participation and engagement, including:

  • Voluntary, community and charitable organisations
  • Youth services and youth groups
  • Schools, colleges and education providers
  • Public sector organisations
  • Health and wellbeing services
  • Social enterprises
  • Independent practitioners and professionals

Stay Connected and Informed

Members of the Bristol Youth Voice Network are added to our mailing list and receive occasional updates, including:

  • Funding and grant opportunities
  • Youth participation and engagement opportunities
  • Training, events and professional development
  • Research, reports and resources
  • Jobs, volunteering and recruitment opportunities
  • Consultations and involvement activities
  • Local, regional and national policy developments

Our Impact

We believe children and young people should have a genuine say in the decisions that shape their lives, communities and futures.

By uniting organisations across Bristol, we are creating a city-wide movement for meaningful youth participation. Through collaboration, shared learning and collective action, we can amplify young people's voices, influence lasting change and build a Bristol where children and young people are recognised as equal partners in creating a better future for all.
