Privacy Notice

This Privacy Notice explains how we collect, use and protect personal information submitted through our website, membership forms, event registration forms and other network activities.

What Information We Collect

We may collect:

  • Name
  • Organisation name
  • Job title or role
  • Email address
  • Information provided through membership applications
  • Information provided when registering for events
  • Information submitted through contact forms, surveys or network activities

We only collect personal information that is necessary to support the operation of the Bristol Youth Voice Network.

Why We Collect Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Manage membership of the Bristol Youth Voice Network
  • Communicate information about network meetings, events and activities
  • Share newsletters, updates and opportunities
  • Register attendees for events
  • Understand interest in network activities
  • Develop and improve network services, events and partnerships

Legal Basis for Processing

We process your personal information on the basis of your consent.

You may withdraw your consent at any time by contacting us using the details below.

How We Collect and Store Information

Google Sites

The Bristol Youth Voice Network website is hosted using Google Sites.

When you visit the website, Google may collect certain technical information such as device information, browser information, IP address and website usage data in accordance with Google's own privacy policies.

For further information, please see:

/privacy" target="_blank"> https://policies.google.com/privacy

Microsoft Forms

Membership applications, event registrations and other information requests are collected using Microsoft Forms.

Information submitted through Microsoft Forms is stored within Microsoft's systems and is accessible only to authorised network administrators.

For further information, please see:

https://privacy.microsoft.com

Who Has Access to Your Information

Information submitted to the Bristol Youth Voice Network will only be accessed by individuals responsible for coordinating and administering network activities.

  • We will not sell your personal information.
  • We will not share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.
  • Where network events are organised jointly with partner organisations, relevant information may be shared where necessary to administer the event. We will make this clear where applicable.

How Long Will We Keep Your Information

We will keep your information only for as long as necessary to support the activities of the Bristol Youth Voice Network.

Information may be reviewed periodically and deleted where it is no longer required.

You may request removal of your information at any time.

Keeping Your Information Secure

We take reasonable steps to ensure that personal information is stored securely and accessed only by authorised individuals involved in coordinating Bristol Youth Voice Network activities.

Information held within Google and Microsoft systems is protected using the security measures provided by those platforms.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to the information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your information
  • Withdraw consent to receive communications
  • Raise concerns about how your information is handled

Changes to This Privacy Notice
