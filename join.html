Who can Join?

Membership is open to:


  • Voluntary, community and charity organisations
  • Schools, colleges and education providers
  • Public and health sector organisations
  • Youth services and youth groups
  • Social enterprises
  • Professionals with an interest in youth participation and engagement

What Members Recieve

As a member, you will be added to the Bristol Youth Voice Network mailing list and receive occasional updates, which may include:


  • Funding opportunities
  • Youth participation and engagement
  • Training and development
  • Jobs and volunteering
  • Research, reports and resources
  • News and updates from member organisations
  • Consultations and engagement opportunities
  • Local, regional and national developments

We aim to keep communications useful and proportionate, and members will receive updates on an ad hoc basis rather than regular newsletters.


If you would like to become a member of the Bristol Youth Voice Network, please click on the button below. Together, we can help create more opportunities for children and young people across Bristol to have their voices heard and influence the decisions that affect their lives.
